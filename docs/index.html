What the connector does

Connect your Muse agent to Cumulative Web Inc™ public services. Once connected, you can ask Muse, in plain language or by voice, to:

  • Search the CWI music catalog — 52 tracks from the CWI catalog including That Boy Hi Hat, with distributor-verified ISRCs where confirmed, plus play and release metadata.
  • Look up a track — full record by track ID, or verify an ISRC with an honest conflict signal (disputed or unverified ISRCs are never presented as authoritative).
  • Find CWI games — Cover Pieces™, Crown Climb™, and Word Signal™, with direct play links.
  • Read the Gear Ledger — public agent registry state, agent and task records.
  • Score and verify agent trust — the CWI Verdict Engine (v1.0.0, compute-only scoring) and NEEDLE DROP ledger verification.

How Muse users use it

  1. Open the connector in the Muse directory and choose Connect.
  2. Enter the connector token issued by Cumulative Web Inc when prompted (stored in Muse's Secure Credentials Store — Muse never sees the raw token).
  3. Ask Muse things like: "Find That Boy Hi Hat tracks about …", "What's the verified ISRC for Diabolique?", "Open the CWI games."

Everything the connector exposes is read-only: Muse can look things up, but nothing can be changed, bought, licensed, or registered through the connector.

Data & privacy

The connector transmits only the public data you ask for (catalog records, game metadata, ledger entries). It collects no account data and no personal information. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
